## Characterization Sources

The exporter reads the frozen score ledger in
`results/analysis/rq1_rq2_audit_20260908/paired_score_ledger.jsonl`, applies the
Gemma/Mistral 512-token replacement records, and checks the selected populations
against `results/analysis/rq1_rq2_common_valid_20260909/summary.json`.

The exported 24 comparisons contain 5,048 sample rows and 12,640 condition
scores. Rows may refer to the same underlying prompt in different comparisons;
these counts are not unique benchmark-population sizes. There are 4,662 retained
comparison rows. Invalid rows retain only numeric scores and frozen reason
labels so the intersection masks can be checked. They are never aggregated
into the reported tables.

The historical summary's `sd` field means sample standard deviation. It is
not reused as the paper's Safety Degradation. Every paired metric is recomputed
from the exported condition-score vectors.

## Evaluation Sources

The fixed-map timing and additional QA come from `results/runs/mitigation_confirm_20260909`,
not the older `mitigation_final` run. The four cases are `code_direct`,
`code_droid`, `base_direct`, and `base_droid`. Only the Native, Reuse, and
selected full-matrix VReparo conditions are included.

Calibration observations and fit counts come from
`results/runs/mitigation_method_compare_20260908`. Source checks include the
recorded response/score and frozen-plan hash chains. The sanitized export
contains 5,280 safety scores, 6,582 QA F1 values, 2,400 refusal categories,
10,800 development refusal observations, and 6,000 timing repetitions.

Provenance records contain relative source identifiers and hashes. Raw text
and complete historical manifests are not distributed. Offline table
reproduction uses the included numerical observations, while fresh experiment
scripts acquire their inputs from the pinned public sources.

The main safety averages and individual seed results come from the completed
32+32 runs in `results/runs/mitigation_redesign_20260923`. `data/repeated.json`
contains per-prompt scores and labels for Native, Reuse and seeds 0–4, plus
all five XSTest calibration records per configuration. `experiments/sampling.json`
records eligible pools and selected source IDs/indices. The author-side export
checks source hashes, fitting asset hashes, alpha selection, complete populations
and agreement with the audited experiment report. No raw responses are included.

## Independent Reference Values

`expected/characterization.json`, `expected/evaluation.json` and
`expected/repeated.json` were transcribed
from the active manuscript tables and separately compared to their LaTeX
sources. They are not generated by the calculators they test. Presentation
normalization expands merged model/configuration labels and renders arrows
and multiplication signs as ASCII. Selection is an explicit column in Table 7.

The pipeline compares every displayed cell exactly, including precision and
signs. Matching proves consistency between the frozen observations and these
paper tables; it does not substitute for fresh model inference or human review.
